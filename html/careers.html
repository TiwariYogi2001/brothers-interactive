// Portfolio

What we look for in a portfolio

Three strong pieces beat fifteen average ones. Here is what our leads check first.

  • Anatomy and proportionEven on stylized work, the underlying structure has to be right. Show a sculpt with clean forms before detail.
  • Game-res breakdownWireframes, UV layout, texture sheets and poly counts. We want to see how the asset was built, not only the beauty render.
  • Material qualityBelievable skin, cloth, metal and leather. Screenshots from Marmoset or an engine, not only a viewport.
  • Style rangeOne realistic and one stylized piece tells us more than five of the same style.
  • FinishComplete assets over half-done experiments. Presentation, lighting and turntables matter.
  • HonestyLabel what was yours on team pieces and which parts used kitbash or scanned data.

// Hiring process

From application to first day

Four steps, usually inside three weeks. We reply to every application.

  1. 01

    Portfolio review

    A lead reviews your ArtStation or PDF within a week and replies either way.

  2. 02

    Art test

    A short, paid test asset for mid and senior roles, scoped to a few days. Interns and juniors get a smaller exercise.

  3. 03

    Interview

    A call with a lead and a member of the leadership team about your process, tools and what you want to work on.

  4. 04

    Offer & onboarding

    Written offer, then a first week paired with a senior on a live project so you ship something early.

// FAQ

Questions applicants ask us

Something else on your mind? Ask in your application message.

Is the work on-site or remote?

The studio is in Jaipur, Rajasthan, and most roles are on-site so juniors can learn beside seniors. Remote or hybrid is considered for experienced artists.

Do I need a degree?

No. The portfolio decides. A degree or diploma helps for internships but is never required for artists with strong work.

Which software should I know?

ZBrush and Maya or Blender for modeling, Substance 3D Painter for texturing, Marmoset Toolbag for presentation. Unreal or Unity familiarity is a plus for every role.

Are art tests paid?

Yes for mid and senior roles. Tests are scoped to a few days of work and we give feedback whether or not we make an offer.

Can I show the work I do here?

Once a game is released and the client approves, yes. Many of the pieces on our portfolio page are production assets shown with permission.

Do you take interns?

Yes, for final-year students and recent graduates with a character-focused portfolio. Internships can lead to full-time roles.
