// Structure

How the team is built

Small pods with a lead artist on every project, so clients always have one person who knows their assets end to end.

  • 01
    Character ArtistsRealistic and stylized humanoids, from sculpt to game-res and textures.
  • 02
    Creature ArtistsAnimals, monsters and stylized creatures with anatomy-driven sculpting.
  • 03
    Prop & Weapon ArtistsHard-surface and organic props, weapons, vehicles and modular kits.
  • 04
    Texture & Hair SpecialistsMaterial authoring, skin and cloth, hair cards and grooming variations.
  • 05
    Riggers & Technical ArtistsSkinning, rigs, LODs and engine integration in Unreal and Unity.
  • 06
    ProductionScheduling, tracking and client communication for every asset in flight.

// Values

What we care about

01

Quality first

Every asset is reviewed by a lead before it leaves the studio. We would rather ask for another day than ship something we are not proud of.

02

On time, every time

Shipped games run on dates. Our schedules have buffer built in and clients hear about risks early, not on the deadline.

03

Artists who grow

Juniors are paired with seniors, portfolio time is protected, and people move up when their work says they are ready.

04

One team with the client

We work inside your tracker, your naming conventions and your review cadence. The goal is to feel in-house.
