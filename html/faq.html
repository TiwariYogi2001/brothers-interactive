// FAQ

Questions producers ask us

Everything a studio usually wants to know before the first call. Anything missing? Ask us directly.

How do you price work?

Two models. Per-asset pricing for defined scopes such as a set of characters or weapons, and a dedicated artist or team on a monthly basis for longer productions. We quote after seeing your concepts and technical spec.

What is the minimum engagement?

There is no minimum. We take on single hero characters as readily as multi-month productions. Small test assets are a common way for new clients to start.

Can you match our existing art style?

Yes. We have shipped work across realistic, stylised and hand-painted styles. Send your style guide and a few in-house reference assets and we match them, usually with a paid test asset first.

How do revisions work?

Feedback checkpoints happen after blockout, sculpt and texture. Revisions within the agreed brief are included. Changes to the brief itself are scoped and quoted separately before we continue.

Do you sign NDAs and who owns the work?

We sign your NDA before any brief is shared. All delivered work is the client's intellectual property. We only show production assets publicly with written approval, so some project details are shared on request rather than on this site.

Which engines and file formats do you deliver?

Unreal Engine and Unity packages, plus FBX and OBJ exports, texture sets in your preferred packing, and source files from ZBrush, Maya, Blender and Substance.

What are your working hours and time zone?

The studio works Indian Standard Time and keeps a daily overlap window with clients in Europe and North America. We respond to feedback within one working day.

How do we start?

Send a short brief with references, asset count, style and deadline through our contact form. We reply with questions, a quote and a proposed schedule, usually within two working days.
