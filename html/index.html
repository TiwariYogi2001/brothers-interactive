// About

Quality and timeline, always.

Brothers Interactive is a team of 3D artists passionate about bringing game characters to life. Since 2019 we've worked as an extension of studios' own art teams, from small indie projects to large AAA productions.

// Services

Eye-catching assets for your game

At Brothers Interactive, we specialize in creating high-quality 3D game art, for the eye-catching assets for your game. Our talented team of artists is comfortable with various quality of game art, ensuring it aligns perfectly with your project requirements.

Specialization

3D Character Design

Realistic and stylized characters taken from concept or reference to game-ready. High-poly sculpt, clean retopology, UVs, bakes and final textures, matched to your art direction and performance budget.

  • Heroes, NPCs, enemies and creatures
  • Realistic, stylized and hand-painted styles
  • Outfit variants, skins and customisation sets
  • Facial and body blendshape support

3D Props & Weapons

Hero props, weapons, vehicles and set dressing that hold up in close-up and stay lean in engine. Built modular where it helps your level team.

  • Firearms, melee and sci-fi weapons
  • Hero props and interactive objects
  • Vehicles and mechanical assets
  • Modular kits and trim sheets

Texturing & Material Creation

PBR texturing, hand-painted stylisation and material authoring. We match your existing library so new assets sit seamlessly next to in-house work.

  • Metal/rough and spec/gloss PBR workflows
  • Hand-painted and stylised textures
  • Skin, hair, cloth and hard-surface materials
  • Texture atlases and channel packing

Skinning & Rigging

Reliable skin weights and production rigs tested against your animation set, delivered ready for Unreal or Unity.

  • Biped, quadruped and creature rigs
  • Skin weighting and deformation passes
  • Facial rig and blendshape setup
  • Engine-ready skeleton conventions

Hair & Grooming

Hair cards and groom variations for realistic characters, with LOD-friendly card layouts and clean alpha and flow maps.

  • Male and female hairstyle sets
  • Beards, brows and lashes
  • Hair card and strand-based workflows
  • Variation packs for customisation

Optimisation & Engine Integration

LOD chains, collision, material setup and import into Unreal Engine or Unity, so assets arrive working rather than as loose files.

  • LOD generation and poly budgets
  • Material instances and shader hookup
  • Unreal and Unity import and validation
  • Naming, folder and export conventions

// Process

How we work

A predictable pipeline with review points at every stage, so you always know where an asset stands.

  1. 01

    Brief & Reference

    We review your concept art, style guide, technical spec and engine targets, then confirm scope, budget and delivery dates.

  2. 02

    Blockout

    Fast proportion and silhouette pass for approval before detail work begins. Catches direction changes early.

  3. 03

    High-poly Sculpt

    Full-detail sculpt with anatomy, cloth and hard-surface detail. Shared as turntables for feedback.

  4. 04

    Retopology & UVs

    Clean, animation-friendly topology to your poly budget, with UV layouts packed for your texel density.

  5. 05

    Baking & Texturing

    Normal, AO and ID bakes, then PBR or hand-painted textures matched to your material library.

  6. 06

    Skinning & Rigging

    Weights and rig built to your skeleton conventions and tested with your animation set.

  7. 07

    Engine Check

    Import into Unreal or Unity, LODs, collision and materials verified in a real scene before handoff.

  8. 08

    Delivery & Support

    Organised source files, exports and a changelog. Revisions and post-delivery fixes are part of the job.

Review roundsFeedback checkpoints after blockout, sculpt and texture. Revisions within the agreed scope are included.
ConfidentialityNDAs signed before any brief is shared. Work is only shown publicly with written client approval.
DeliverablesZBrush/Maya/Blender source, FBX/OBJ exports, texture sets, Marmoset scenes and engine-ready packages on request.
CommunicationDedicated point of contact, weekly status updates and a shared tracker for every asset in production.

// Styles

Four looks, one pipeline

Most briefs fall into one of these styles. Find yours, see what we have shipped in it, and tell us which one when you send a brief.

// 01

Realistic

AAA-grade humans and creatures: anatomy-first sculpting, scanned-quality skin detail, layered materials and hair cards that read at close range. This is the style behind our work on tactical shooters and action titles.

  • PBR metal/rough, 4K texture sets, tertiary skin detail
  • Hair, cloth and gear as separate optimised meshes
  • Best for: shooters, action, simulation, cinematics
See realistic humans →

// 02

Stylized

Clean, appealing shapes with exaggerated proportions and readable silhouettes. Surfaces stay simple so the character reads at any distance, and rigs are built for expressive animation.

  • Strong silhouette and shape language passes
  • Gradient and light-baked or simplified PBR materials
  • Best for: adventure, family, mobile, competitive titles
See stylized work →

// 03

Hand-painted & gothic

Painterly texture work where light and material are brushed into the diffuse, as in our Lost in Random characters. Great where a distinctive, illustrative identity matters more than photoreal detail.

  • Painted diffuse with light PBR support
  • Low texture budgets, strong art direction match
  • Best for: story-driven, indie, distinctive IPs
See the Lost in Random cast →

// 04

Claymation & sculpted

Characters that keep the fingerprints and tool marks of physical clay, translated into game meshes without losing the handmade feel. Developed with MoonHood for The Midnight Walk.

  • Sculpt from physical or photographed clay reference
  • Surface imperfection and stop-motion friendly rigs
  • Best for: dark fairy tales, horror, art-led projects
See The Midnight Walk characters →
