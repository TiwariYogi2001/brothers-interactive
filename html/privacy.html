Privacy policy

Last updated: September 2026. Please review this text with your legal adviser before publishing.

Who we are

Brothers Interactive is a 3D game art studio based in Jaipur, Rajasthan, India. This site is operated by the studio. Contact: business@brothersinteractive.com.

What we collect

  • Contact and brief forms. Name, email address, studio name and the project details you type in. These are sent to us by email or through our form provider so we can reply.
  • Job applications. Name, email, role, portfolio link, experience level and your message. We keep applications on file for up to 12 months so we can contact you when a role opens.
  • Analytics. If enabled, we use a privacy-friendly analytics service that records page views and events without cookies and without identifying individual visitors.
  • Theme preference. Your chosen colour theme is stored in your own browser only. It is never sent to us.

How we use it

To answer your enquiry, prepare a quote, evaluate an application, and understand which parts of the site are useful. We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Third parties

  • Form submissions may be processed by Formspree if configured. Video trailers are embedded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode. Interactive 3D viewers, where present, are embedded from Sketchfab. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Each of these services has its own privacy policy.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete any information you have sent us. Email business@brothersinteractive.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page is updated.
