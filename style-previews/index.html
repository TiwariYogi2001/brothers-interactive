Style previews

These are standalone previews of the homepage in three alternate visual styles. They are separate copies — nothing in html/, css/style.css or js/ was changed to make these. The existing colour-theme switcher (bottom-left on each preview) still works normally.

Original

The current live site, unchanged, for comparison.

Neo-Brutalist

Square corners, thick borders, hard offset shadows, no glow. Bold and graphic.

Frosted Glass

Translucent blurred cards, soft large corners, layered depth. Airy and modern.

Studio Editorial

No neon glow or grid, quiet borders, refined serif headings. Calm and portfolio-like.

To remove these previews entirely: delete the style-previews/ and css/variants/ folders. Nothing else is affected.
